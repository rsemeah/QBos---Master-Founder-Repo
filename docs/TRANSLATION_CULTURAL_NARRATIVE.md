# QuietBuild OS — We Built the System That Can Say No

Most systems chase power. We chased honesty.

QuietBuild is constitutional infrastructure for AI that refuses power when the truth is uncertain. It stops instead of guessing. It documents instead of obscuring. It answers to people.

## The Line That Matters

When your AI says it's confident, ask: **who can stop it?**

In QuietBuild, the answer is simple: **you can**.

- Robby can propose, but cannot approve its own constraints.
- Locked zones cannot be touched.
- Production cannot run without real safeguards.
- Refusals are explicit, logged, and auditable.

## What We Proved

- Power with brakes: Phase 4 refuses without HSM/Vault, nonce ledger, verifier.
- Governance without loopholes: Phase 5 requires human + auditor approval; Robby abstains.
- Truth without theatrics: Receipts for every action; witness artifacts for refusal.
