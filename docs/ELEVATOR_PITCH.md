# QuietBuild OS — Elevator Pitch

**QuietBuild OS (QBOS)** is a constitutional, TruthSerum-first platform for building software where nothing is allowed to "work" without proof. It's made of eight engines—execution, identity, consent, configuration, billing logic, notifications, visual quality, and AI routing—coordinated by a deterministic state machine. Every action emits receipts, every claim is marked *Verified*, *Implemented*, or *Unknown*, and CI blocks anything that can't be proven. The infrastructure is complete and compiling; some external integrations (deploy, billing, full autonomous generation) are intentionally marked *Unknown* until end-to-end receipts exist. QBOS is built for teams who want speed **without** black boxes, demos **without** hand-waving, and progress that can be audited, shared, and trusted.
